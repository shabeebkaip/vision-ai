# ADR 005: WhatsApp is the primary alert channel

Status: accepted (2 Nov 2026)

**Decision.** Alerts go to WhatsApp (Cloud API, utility templates with an image and "Correct" /
"False alarm" buttons). Telegram is the development and fallback channel; email for summaries.

**Why.** Saudi managers live in WhatsApp; a utility message costs about SAR 0.04, and replies inside
24 hours open a free window. The buttons give us the feedback that measures and improves accuracy.
