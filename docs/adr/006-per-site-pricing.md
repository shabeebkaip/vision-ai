# ADR 006: Per-site plans drive camera limits

Status: accepted (2 Nov 2026)

**Decision.** Customers buy per-site plans (Starter 4 cameras SAR 299, Standard 8 SAR 499,
Business 16 SAR 899, Enterprise 32 SAR 1,499 per month) plus a one-time setup fee. The software
enforces the camera limit per plan (CLD-18); hardware tiers are sized to those limits.
